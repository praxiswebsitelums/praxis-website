About this notice

This Privacy Notice applies to the Praxis Initiative website hosted at praxis.lums.edu.pk. It describes the website analytics described below. It does not replace any privacy notice or policy that may apply to Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS) or to other websites and services linked from this website.

Website analytics

The website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), deployed through Google Tag Manager, to help us understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content, performance and visitor experience.

Analytics information is used to understand general patterns, such as which pages are visited and how visitors arrive at and interact with the website. We do not use website analytics to make decisions about individual visitors.

Information that may be collected

Depending on the analytics configuration and your browser settings, Google Analytics may collect or generate information such as:

  • Pages viewed, interactions and approximate time spent on the website;
  • The website or link that referred you to Praxis;
  • Browser, operating system, device and screen information;
  • Approximate geographic information, such as country or city; and
  • Online identifiers and information stored in or read from cookies or similar technologies.

The information available to us is generally presented as website usage and statistical reports. The exact information collected depends on the tags and settings enabled in Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish browsers and measure website usage. You can manage, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you block cookies, some website or analytics functions may not work as intended.

You can also learn about Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Consent and regional requirements: Cookie and analytics consent requirements depend on applicable law and the website's audience. Where consent is required, analytics technologies should not be activated until the visitor has given the required consent. This notice itself is not a consent mechanism.

Google services

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Information collected through Google Analytics is processed by Google in accordance with its own terms and policies. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Contacting Praxis by email

The website provides email links for visitors who wish to contact Praxis. If you choose to email us, the information you include in your message will be handled through the email service you use and the recipient's email arrangements. Please avoid including sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when the website, its analytics configuration or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

For questions about this notice or the website, please contact Praxis through the Contact Us page.
